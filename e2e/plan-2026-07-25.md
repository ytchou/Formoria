# E2E Author Plan — 2026-07-25
**Input:** diff (`feat/brand-directory-feedback`, including the dirty worktree UI changes)
**Scope:** brand detail actions, FAQ presentation changes, global heading treatment, and admin-menu placement

## Journeys to author

### 1. Anonymous visitor supports and unsupports a brand  [NEW spec]
- **Actor:** anonymous visitor
- **Goal:** encourage a brand from its public detail page and undo that support.
- **Pre-conditions:** service-role seeded approved `[E2E-TEST]` brand with a unique slug; fresh anonymous browser context.
- **Steps:** open the brand page; wait for the support count; click the support button; verify the pressed state and count increment; click again and verify the count returns to zero.
- **Expected (observable):** the action is available without sign-in, exposes the localized support count through its label, and toggles `aria-pressed` with the count.
- **Risk if broken:** visitors cannot provide the new public encouragement signal or receive stale feedback after toggling it.
- **DB/auth state:** `[E2E-TEST]` self-seeded brand; anonymous visitor cookie and `brand_likes` rows are cleaned by brand cascade/global teardown.
- **Target project:** deep (Chromium-only)
- **Destination spec:** `e2e/tests/brand-actions.spec.ts`

### 2. Anonymous visitor receives sign-in guidance for evidence  [NEW spec]
- **Actor:** anonymous visitor
- **Goal:** understand that origin evidence requires authentication and reach sign-in without losing the current brand context.
- **Pre-conditions:** the same approved `[E2E-TEST]` brand; anonymous browser context.
- **Steps:** open the brand page; open “回報產地資訊”; inspect the dialog copy and sign-in link; cancel the dialog.
- **Expected (observable):** the “提供產地證據” dialog shows the localized sign-in prompt, links to `/auth/sign-in?next=...`, and closes through “取消”.
- **Risk if broken:** anonymous visitors may submit unusable evidence or be stranded without a return path.
- **DB/auth state:** no mutation; anonymous browser context and `[E2E-TEST]` brand cleanup.
- **Target project:** deep (Chromium-only)
- **Destination spec:** `e2e/tests/brand-actions.spec.ts`

### 3. Admin reaches the menu from the right side of the brand heading  [NEW spec]
- **Actor:** authenticated administrator
- **Goal:** locate brand management actions in the heading row without confusing them with public actions.
- **Pre-conditions:** E2E admin storage state; service-role seeded approved `[E2E-TEST]` brand; desktop Chromium viewport.
- **Steps:** open the brand page as admin; locate “管理選單”; compare its position with the level-1 brand heading.
- **Expected (observable):** the menu button is visible on the same heading row and its left edge is at or beyond the h1 right edge.
- **Risk if broken:** admin controls remain buried in the public action group or are visually detached from the brand identity.
- **DB/auth state:** admin storage state; self-seeded `[E2E-TEST]` brand, cleaned after the run.
- **Target project:** deep (Chromium-only)
- **Destination spec:** `e2e/tests/brand-actions.spec.ts`
