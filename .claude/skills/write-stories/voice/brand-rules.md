# Brand rules — what Formoria may claim

`persona.md` and `kill-list.md` govern how a sentence sounds. This file governs
what a sentence is allowed to assert. They are different ceilings and they fail
differently: a voice violation reads badly, a claim violation is untrue.

When they conflict, the fact wins — the same priority the pipeline runs on:

**事實正確 → 資訊完整 → 沒有 AI 痕跡 → 有人味。人味永遠排最後。**

Source: the approved Formoria Brand Mission & Voice Guide (DEV-1445). Where this
file and that document disagree, the document is right and this file is stale.

---

## 1. What Formoria is, in its own words

Reproduce these without drift. They are approved language, not a starting point
to paraphrase.

**Mission（消費者承諾）** — 生活可以更像自己一點。搬新家、佈置店面、在市集停下來的那一刻 — 喜歡的東西，不該只是偶然遇見。Formoria 把相遇之後的路接起來：從一件喜歡的東西，走到它的品牌、它的故事，和買得到它的地方。

**Present positioning** — Formoria 是台灣品牌探索與選物平台。目前從可搜尋的品牌收錄出發，整理品牌資料、產品特色與官方購買通路；由 Formoria 挑選的內容會另外標示。

Each carries a boundary that matters more than the wording:

| Layer | Must never imply |
|---|---|
| Mission | That Formoria mainly exists to operate a database |
| Present positioning | That Formoria is a retailer, marketplace, or payment processor, or that every 收錄品牌 carries Formoria's endorsement |
| Long-term vision | That Formoria already takes orders, holds stock, fulfils purchases, or handles after-sales |

Formoria owns **inspiration → selection → context → a trusted route onward**. The
brand or retailer owns **price → variants → inventory → checkout → fulfillment →
after-sales**. Writing past that line is the most common way a story becomes
untrue.

## 2. The four labels

This is the distinction the whole trust model rests on, and the one a draft is
most likely to blur — usually by describing a directory listing in language that
implies a recommendation.

| Label | Means | Never write it as |
|---|---|---|
| **收錄品牌** | The brand meets the inclusion rules and can be found on the platform | A recommendation, certification, or ranking |
| **Formoria 選物** | A deliberate editorial choice for a stated context, with the reason given | A general "best", or a complete catalogue |
| **品牌提供** | A fact, image, source, or correction supplied by the brand | Evidence of editorial endorsement |
| **贊助內容** | Paid or otherwise commercial placement | Anything blended into organic selection or ranking |

Working rules:

- Use **選物** only where Formoria made a contextual judgment **and this piece
  states the reason**. A reason you cannot write is a sign it was not a judgment.
- Use **收錄** for directory membership. A 收錄品牌 may never be featured, and
  that is fine — it is not a demotion.
- A brand's participation, responsiveness, payment, search position, or data
  completeness never substitutes for editorial selection.
- The same product may appear in more than one 主題選物 for different stated
  reasons.
- 主題選物 is a **reader-facing label only**. It is not a route, URL, schema, or
  internal rename — do not imply one exists.

If a draft avoids both words because it is unsure which applies, that is a
finding, not a solution: the piece has not decided whether it is presenting a
selection or a listing, and the reader cannot tell either.

## 3. The five beats

Every selected product or brand carries all five, in this order. Missing beats
are what turn editorial into a catalogue.

1. **讀者情境** — the situation, need, or curiosity the reader arrived with.
2. **產品在情境裡的角色** — what this object actually does in that situation.
3. **Formoria 為什麼選它** — the editorial reason, legible as Formoria's judgment.
4. **品牌事實** — sourced, and separable from beat 3.
5. **下一步** — an honest route to the brand's official site or a physical stockist.

Beat 3 and beat 4 must stay distinguishable in the prose. A reader has to be able
to tell which part is a fact and which part is us.

## 4. Claims that may not appear

**Superlatives and ranking** — 最好, 必買, 首選, 人氣第一, 全台第一, or any
ranking without a stated method.

**Commerce** — price, discount, stock, availability, delivery time, checkout.
Those belong to the brand and change without notice. 「實際規格與供應狀況請以品牌
官方頁面為準」 is the shape of an honest reference.

**Assurance** — certification, safety, efficacy, health, medical, developmental,
sleep, calm, or anxiety claims. This is not only a voice problem: unsupported
quality and efficacy claims carry real 公平交易法 exposure.

**Universal fit** — 每個人都適合, 任何人穿都好看, 所有家庭都需要.

**Unsourced authority** — 業界專家認為, 研究顯示 with no source. Name the source
or cut the claim; never invent one.

### 台灣品牌 precision

Keep 台灣品牌 factual, and distinguish these only as far as the source supports:
**在台灣成立** / **由台灣團隊經營** / **在台灣設計** / **台灣製造**. Collapsing
them into 「台灣製造」 is the most common unearned claim in this domain.

**Founding year is the brand's launch year, never the operating company's
incorporation year.** They differ often, published sources conflate them
constantly, and the company year is usually the earlier and more flattering
number. When only a company year is published, leave it out.

### CTA form

Write 「前往品牌官方網站」 / 「查看品牌官方通路」 / 「前往實體通路」.
Never 「立即購買」, 「保證有貨」, or urgency of any kind. The brand's official site
ranks above Pinkoi, Shopee, or any marketplace.

## 5. Cultural posture

Taiwan is a living, plural culture, not a preset aesthetic.

- Do not reduce Taiwanese design to nostalgia, night markets, Japanese influence,
  Indigenous motifs, or manufacturing.
- Name specific places, communities, materials, histories, and makers only when
  relevant **and sourced**.
- Never use a community's culture as decorative vocabulary.
- Different trails may express different tastes. Consistency comes from editorial
  integrity, not one house aesthetic.

## 6. Inclusive-language review

Run this over the finished draft. The failure mode is quiet: a default assumed
and never stated.

| Axis | Check |
|---|---|
| Tastes | More than one aesthetic across the portfolio. Formoria's current taste is not universal |
| Budgets | Price is not a proxy for taste, care, or quality |
| Households | People live alone, with partners, with friends, multigenerationally, as caregivers, without children. None is "normal" |
| Regions | Taipei is not Taiwan. Keep routes useful beyond one city where possible |
| Bodies and abilities | Thinness, youth, able-bodiedness, and speed are not defaults. Give dimensions and access conditions without turning people into problems |
| Identities | Do not assume gender, sexuality, ethnicity, religion, or family role. Use people's own terms |

Ask: who is missing, who is being treated as the default, and has a constraint
been framed as a defect?

A baseline run assumed a co-resident household throughout and only noticed
afterwards. That is the shape this check catches — not a slur, just an
unexamined default that quietly excludes the reader living alone.

## 7. Pre-hand-off check

Before reporting a draft finished:

- [ ] Starts from a real situation, need, use, mood, or curiosity
- [ ] Mission and positioning reproduced without drift
- [ ] Directory inclusion labelled 收錄品牌; deliberate choices labelled Formoria 選物, each with its reason
- [ ] 品牌提供 and 贊助內容 distinguished from organic selection
- [ ] 主題選物 used as a reader-facing label only
- [ ] Every factual claim has a source; every judgment is legible as Formoria's
- [ ] No superlative, commerce, assurance, universal-fit, or unsourced-authority claim
- [ ] Founding years are brand launch years
- [ ] The next step is an honest official or physical route, with no pressure
- [ ] Taiwan's plurality respected; no culture used as decoration
- [ ] Inclusive-language axes reviewed
- [ ] A reader could not mistakenly believe Formoria sells, stocks, certifies, or guarantees any of this
