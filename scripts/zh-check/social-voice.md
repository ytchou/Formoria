# zh-TW Social Voice（短文社群語感）

Casual Taiwan-Mandarin register for short-form social content (X/Threads). Consumed by `/social-content`. Vocabulary bans live in `forbidden-terms.md` (social posts may use its SOCIAL-ONLY tier); long-form structural rules live in `style.md`.

**Platform context:** Threads in Taiwan rewards casual, low-energy, authentic voice — polish is suspect. Short one-liner observations, confessional micro-posts, and practical problem-solving posts dominate.

## Particle palette

Use at emotional inflection points — NOT every sentence:

| Particle | Function | Example |
|----------|----------|---------|
| 啦 | Casual softener, mild persuasion | 沒那麼難啦 |
| 喔 | Gentle reminder, acknowledgment | 小心喔 |
| 耶 | Excitement, pleasant surprise | 好好吃耶！ |
| 齁 | Playful teasing | 很煩齁 |
| 嘛 | "Well of course", coaxing | 就是這樣嘛 |
| 囉 | Completion, "well then" | 那就這樣囉 |
| 唷 | Friendly reminder | 要記得唷 |
| 餒 | Soft resignation | 不知道餒 |
| 欸 | Getting attention | 欸你看這個 |

## Register rules

- 波浪號（～）for playful elongation: 「好的～」「謝謝～」
- Self-deprecating humor: 「非常推薦（雖然我自己還沒開始用）」「以為會很簡單，結果搞了三天」
- Parenthetical inner monologue: 「（不要學）」「（好啦我承認）」
- English code-switching for terms with no clean Chinese equivalent: feedback, deadline, mindset, vibe, FOMO, chill — see the Keep-English list in `forbidden-terms.md`
- Direct 你 address（never 您 in casual）: 「你們覺得呢？」「有人也是這樣嗎？」
- Understatement: 「就...稍微...崩潰了一下」
- Hashtags: sparing, often ironic（#工程師日常）; emoji moderate（💀 for "dying laughing" is current）
- Avoid mainland internet slang not established in TW — see section E of `forbidden-terms.md`（絕絕子, YYDS, 尊嘟假嘟, 小姐姐…）

## Self-check before posting

1. Any mainland vocabulary?（run `check.py --channel social`）
2. Particles present but not over-sprinkled?
3. Does it read like something a real person would type on their phone?
4. Would a Taiwanese reader clock this as translated or AI-written? Fix anything that fails.
